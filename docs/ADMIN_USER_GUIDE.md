# JaniduDev Website Manager — Simple User Guide

Welcome to your private JaniduDev Website Manager! You never need to write or touch any code to run your portfolio, add new industries, approve reviews, or track your freelance clients.

---

## 1. Signing In & Staying Safe
1. Visit **`/login`** on your website.
2. Enter your email address and password, then click **Sign In**.
3. If you turned on **Two-Step Verification**, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (or click **Email Code** to receive one in your inbox).
4. **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen to receive a 6-digit reset code by email.
5. **To sign out**, click **Log Out** at the bottom-left of the sidebar.

---

## 2. Overview Screen
When you sign in, **Overview** shows:
- How many projects, categories, reviews, and client builds you have.
- **Needs Attention** alerts whenever a new customer enquiry arrives or a new review is waiting for your approval.
- **Quick Actions** to jump straight to common tasks.
- **View Website** (top-right or bottom-left) to open your live public site in a new tab.

---

## 3. Adding a New Industry Category (e.g., Restaurants, Hotels, Cleaning)
1. Click **Categories** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Category**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Category Name** (e.g., `Restaurants`)
   - **Section Title** (e.g., `Modern Restaurant Websites`)
   - **Description** (a short friendly summary)
   - Click **Choose or Upload Image** to add a cover photo.
4. Set **Publish Status** to **Published**, check **Show on public website**, and click **Create & Update Website**.
5. Your new industry showcase immediately appears on your live website at `/categories/restaurants`!
6. Use the **Up / Down arrows** to rearrange categories. Deleting a category never deletes its projects — it asks whether to move them to another category or keep them uncategorized.

---

## 4. Adding or Editing a Portfolio Project
1. Click **Projects → Add Project**.
2. Follow the 4 simple steps:
   - **1. Details**: Name, category, type (**Real Client Project**, **Demo Website**, **Design Concept**, or **Personal Project**), short summary, and key features built.
   - **2. Images**: Upload or pick a cover image and extra desktop/mobile screenshots.
   - **3. Links**: Add the live website link, demo link, or custom buttons.
   - **4. Preview & Publish**: Choose whether to feature it on the homepage, then click **Save as Draft** or **Publish to Website**.
3. You can also **Duplicate** a project, star it as **Featured**, or move it to **Trash** (and **Restore** it later if needed).

---

## 5. Approving Customer Reviews
1. Click **Customer Reviews** in the sidebar.
2. New reviews submitted on your website land in **Awaiting Approval** — they do not appear publicly until you approve them.
3. Click **Approve & Publish** to show a review on your live website, **Hide** to take it offline, or the **Star** icon to highlight it on your homepage.
4. Click **Add Testimonial** to manually add a genuine review a client sent you over WhatsApp or email.

---

## 6. Managing Customer Enquiries & Active Clients
1. Click **Customer Enquiries** to read messages from your website contact form.
2. Reply directly via **Reply by Email** or **Open WhatsApp**, update the enquiry status, or write private notes.
3. When a lead becomes a paying client, click **Convert to Client** — this automatically creates their private client profile and their first website project!
4. Under **Clients & Projects**, open any client to:
   - Track their project stage (`Proposal Sent` → `50% Deposit Paid` → `In Development` → `Private Preview Ready` → `Revisions` → `Final 50% Paid` → `Launched`).
   - Check off **50% Deposit Paid**, **Final 50% Paid**, and delivery milestones.
   - Save their private preview link and final live site URL.
   - Click **Copy to Portfolio Showcase** after launch to turn it into a draft portfolio project in one click!

---

## 7. Updating Website Content, Services & Settings
- **Website Content**: Edit homepage text or add an **Announcement Banner** or custom call-to-action section.
- **Services**: Add optional service cards and use the master **Show Services section on public website** toggle whenever you want them shown or hidden.
- **Media Library**: Upload, search, label, or remove images and videos. Images currently used by a project are protected from accidental deletion.
- **Settings**: Update your public contact email, WhatsApp number, email notification preferences, Two-Step Verification, and see or sign out other **Signed-In Devices**.
